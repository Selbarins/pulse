## One-Line Pitch

A single-player life RPG where real-world actions in money, health, time and projects generate XP, levels, multipliers and tangible rewards. The only score that matters is measurable ROI — more money, better health, sharper focus and visible progress.

This app has exactly one user. Everything is built around his reality.

| **Investments** | None yet | €500/mo habit by end of Season 1 |

## Core Fantasy

You are the player character.
Every real action you take feeds the game.
The game tracks hard stats, awards XP and levels, applies temporary and permanent multipliers, and turns discipline into visible progression and real-life gains.

No philosophical journaling. No soft life-admin.
Pure performance loop with **money** and **health** as the primary currencies.

## Primary Goals (ROI — in priority order)

1. **Wealth** — Higher surplus, first investments, net-worth growth, business foundation
2. **Vitality** — Consistent training, blood sugar control, weight trend, sleep quality

4. **Momentum** — Project milestones, weekly wins, habit adherence

## Character Stats (The Five Attributes)

### 💰 Wealth
Tracks financial health and forward momentum.
- Monthly income logged
- Monthly surplus (income − spending)
- Investment balance and monthly contribution
- Spending categories and leakage identified
- Net-worth snapshot (monthly)
- Business idea progress (qualitative milestone tracker)

**Level unlocks:** better surplus tools, investment dashboards, business planning modules

---

### ❤️ Vitality
Tracks physical health — with diabetic-aware design built in.
- Training sessions (type, duration, intensity)
- Daily glycemia reading (morning fasted as primary, post-meal optional)
- Daily weight (weekly average is the meaningful number)
- Sleep duration and subjective quality score (1–5)
- Daily energy score (1–5, self-reported)

> Glycemia and training are linked: resistance training improves insulin sensitivity. The game reflects this — consistent training applies a passive Vitality multiplier and a secondary Wealth buff (lower health costs, higher energy → higher output).

**Level unlocks:** body composition metrics, HRV tracking readiness, advanced training splits

---

### 🎯 Focus
Tracks cognitive output quality.
- Deep work blocks logged (hours, topic)
- Task completion rate (planned vs done)
- Low-value time flagged (optional, honest logging)
- Business idea time tracked separately

**Level unlocks:** Pomodoro/time-block tools, project decomposition systems, focus streak bonuses

---

### 🚀 Momentum
Tracks project and life progress.
- Weekly milestones hit vs missed
- Active projects (start with 1: business direction)
- Weekly wins log (small but real)
- Habit adherence rate

**Level unlocks:** project boards, milestone forecasting, momentum compounding multipliers

---

### 🔒 Discipline
Meta-attribute. Derived from consistency across all other stats.
- Daily quest completion streak
- Weekly boss completion rate
- Logging consistency score
- No manual input — calculated automatically

**Level unlocks:** multiplier amplifiers on all other attributes, prestige system access

## Game Systems

### 1. Daily Quests
Personalized to the player's actual life. Fast to log (target: under 60 seconds per quest).

**Core daily quests (Season 1 baseline):**

| Quest | Attribute | XP |
|---|---|---|
| Log morning glycemia | Vitality | 50 |
| Log weight | Vitality | 30 |
| Log sleep (duration + quality score) | Vitality | 40 |
| Log daily energy score | Vitality | 20 |
| Complete a training session | Vitality | 150 |
| Log all spending for the day | Wealth | 80 |
| Complete 1 deep-work block (≥45 min) on business | Focus | 120 |
| Make one intentional surplus/investment decision | Wealth | 100 |

**Total daily XP available (no training day):** ~390
**Total daily XP available (training day):** ~540

---

### 2. Weekly Bosses
Bigger targets. Real rewards on success. Debt state on failure.

**Season 1 Weekly Bosses:**

| Boss | Condition | Reward | Failure Penalty |
|---|---|---|---|
| Iron Will | 3+ training sessions completed | +500 XP + Vitality multiplier tick | -10% XP on Vitality quests next week |
| Surplus Lock | Spent less than income, surplus > 0 | Unlock discretionary spend amount | Surplus locked — extra goes to savings |
| Deep Work Boss | 5+ deep work blocks in the week | +400 XP + Focus multiplier tick | Focus XP halved next week |
| The Log | 7/7 days of full daily logging | +300 XP + Discipline streak bonus | Streak resets |
| Glycemia Guardian | ≥5 readings logged, no critical highs | +200 XP + health insight unlocked | Warning flag on dashboard |

---

### 3. The Compound Interest Mechanic
Streaks don't just give XP — they apply a **multiplier that grows exponentially then crashes on break.**

| Streak Length | Multiplier |
|---|---|
| 3–6 days | 1.1× |
| 7–13 days | 1.25× |
| 14–20 days | 1.5× |
| 21–29 days | 1.75× |
| 30+ days | 2.0× (cap) |

Breaking the streak resets to 1.0×. Recovering takes half the original time.
This makes the streak feel like a real financial asset — because it is.

---

### 4. Anti-Inflation / Scaling Difficulty
Quests automatically scale as attributes level up. The game tracks your rolling 4-week average and adjusts targets upward.

- Week 1: 2 training sessions qualifies for Iron Will
- Month 3: 3 sessions required
- Month 6: 4 sessions + minimum intensity threshold

The player is always playing slightly above their comfort zone. Stagnation doesn't maintain XP rate — it slowly reduces it.

---

### 5. Debt States
Failed Weekly Boss → enter **Debt State** for the following week.
- A portion of new XP (25%) is diverted to "paying off" the missed boss
- Dashboard shows a red debt indicator until cleared
- Clears automatically when debt XP is repaid
- Does not stack catastrophically — max debt is one boss at a time

---

### 6. The Edge System (Daily Variable Multipliers)
Each morning at reset, one random Edge event activates:

Examples:
- "Fasted training today gives +40% Vitality XP"
- "Any surplus decision over X amount gets double Wealth XP"
- "First deep-work block today earns +50% Focus XP"
- "Log glycemia before 8am for bonus Vitality XP"

Purpose: freshness, reason to check the app first thing, light daily excitement.

---

### 7. Real-Money Economy
The money system has real teeth.

- **Player Budget:** monthly income is logged and split into categories
- **Locked Capital:** money not yet "earned" through performance stays locked — defaults to savings
- **Unlocked Capital:** earned through hitting weekly bosses and surplus targets — becomes discretionary spend
- **Investment Pipeline:** strong monthly performance auto-directs a target % to investment
- **Wealth XP from investment gains:** once investments are live, real gains generate bonus XP

> Goal: by end of Season 1 (Day 90), a real investment account exists with something in it.

---

### 8. Action Points (AP)
Daily pool of Action Points representing cognitive and physical capacity.

- Base pool: 100 AP/day
- Sleep quality modifier: poor sleep (score 1–2) → 70 AP. Good sleep (4–5) → 120 AP
- High-leverage actions (deep work, training) cost AP efficiently
- Low-value activities can be logged as AP drains
- Focus attribute improves AP efficiency over time (same actions cost fewer AP)

---

### 9. The Shadow Self
A ghost character built from the player's own historical best week.
- Always visible on the dashboard as a comparison benchmark
- Not external — not some ideal person, not an average user
- Racing your own peak, not a leaderboard
- Updates every 30 days to reflect new capabilities

---

### 10. Return on Action (ROA) Log
After a real outcome (closed income source, lost weight, saved X amount), player logs it.
System reverse-calculates which habits and quests drove it.
Over months, builds a personal ROA table — which actions actually move which stats.
This becomes the most valuable personal dataset the app generates.

## Configuration Layer

> **Core principle: Nothing is hardcoded. Everything is yours to change, anytime, without penalty.**

Goals evolve. Life changes trajectory. The app must flex with the player, not trap him in yesterday's ambitions. The Configuration Layer is the system that makes Pulse permanently adaptive.

---

### What Is Always Editable

Every target, threshold, and active quest lives in the Player Config — a dedicated settings screen. Changing anything here takes effect immediately going forward. History is never rewritten.

**Wealth Config**
- Monthly income target
- Monthly surplus target (can be set to any amount, including 0 during hard months)
- Monthly investment contribution target (starts at 0, grows when ready)
- Spending category budgets (add, remove, rename categories freely)
- Which Wealth quests are active this season
- Investment vehicle preference (index funds, savings, business fund — player decides)

**Vitality Config**
- Training frequency target per week (1×, 2×, 3×, 4×, 5× — player sets)
- Training type (strength, cardio, mixed — affects quest framing)
- Glycemia target range (set in consultation with doctor — the app never overrides this)
- Weight goal direction (lose / maintain / gain — changes how weight logs are scored)
- Which Vitality quests are active this season

**Focus Config**
- Daily deep work block target (duration in minutes)
- Deep work topic tags (what counts as "business work" vs "job work")
- Task completion rate target (%)
- Which Focus quests are active this season

**Momentum Config**
- Active projects list (add, remove, rename anytime)
- Current business stage (Idea → Research → Validated → Building → Revenue)
- Weekly milestone targets per project

**Season Config**
- Season duration (default 90 days, extendable to 120 or shortened to 60)
- Season name (player-defined — "Activate", "Rebuild", "Launch", whatever fits)
- Season success criteria (player writes their own 3–5 targets at season start)
- Season 1 boss evaluation threshold (default: 4/5 targets, adjustable)

**Quest Config**
- Any quest can be toggled on/off
- Quest XP values are fixed by the system (prevents gaming), but active/inactive is player-controlled
- Custom quests can be added with a name, attribute tag, and XP value (capped at 100 XP for custom)

**Weekly Boss Config**
- Each boss can be toggled active/inactive per season
- Boss thresholds are editable (e.g. Iron Will: change from 3 sessions to 2 during injury)
- Boss rewards and penalties scale automatically with the new threshold

---

### Two Types of Changes

The system distinguishes between two different kinds of edits, handling each appropriately:

#### Type 1: Calibration
*Small adjustment to stay honest with reality.*

Examples:
- Surplus target drops from 3,000 to 1,500 after an unexpected expense month
- Training frequency drops from 3× to 2× during a busy work period
- Investment target stays at 0 while building an emergency fund first

**How the system handles it:**
- Config updates immediately
- Past data is untouched — history shows what actually happened
- No XP penalty, no guilt mechanic
- Dashboard shows new targets from today forward
- A small neutral log entry is created: *"Surplus target recalibrated — [date]"*

---

#### Type 2: Pivot
*Meaningful change in direction. A real decision, not just a tweak.*

Examples:
- Shifting investment strategy from index funds to a business savings fund
- Changing the business stage from "Research" to "Building"
- Switching training type from cardio to strength
- Deprioritizing Focus entirely for a month to focus on health recovery

**How the system handles it:**
- Player writes a short note (1–3 sentences) explaining the pivot — optional but encouraged
- A **Pivot Event** is logged with timestamp and note
- Pivot Events appear as markers on trend graphs — visible moments in your own story
- Config updates immediately after the pivot is logged
- History preserved in full — the pivot is visible, not erased

> The Pivot Log becomes one of the most valuable things the app generates over time. Looking back at when and why you changed direction is more useful than any single metric.

---

### What Never Changes

These are permanent. They do not reset, cannot be turned off, and are never affected by config changes:

- **The five attributes** (Wealth, Vitality, Focus, Momentum, Discipline) — always tracked
- **XP and level history** — cumulative, never wiped
- **Daily log data** — every entry is permanent record
- **Streak history** — including breaks, shown on timeline
- **Pivot Event log** — immutable once written
- **Season history** — each completed season is archived with its results

The config changes the *targets*. The attributes track *reality*. These are two separate things by design.

### Database Addition: Config & Pivot Tables

```sql
-- Player config (single active row per attribute domain)
player_config (
  id,
  domain,                  -- 'wealth' | 'vitality' | 'focus' | 'momentum' | 'season'
  config_json,             -- all editable fields for that domain as JSON
  updated_at
)

-- Config change log (append-only, never deleted)
config_change_log (
  id,
  changed_at,
  domain,
  field_changed,
  old_value,
  new_value,
  change_type              -- 'calibration' | 'pivot'
)

-- Pivot events (player-authored direction changes)
pivot_events (
  id,
  pivot_date,
  domain,                  -- which attribute area this pivot affects
  title,                   -- short label e.g. "Shifted to business savings"
  note,                    -- player's own words, optional
  created_at
)
```

### Design Rule

> **The app never tells the player their goal was wrong. It only tracks what the player decided, when they decided it, and what happened as a result.**

Pulse is a mirror, not a judge. Config changes are a sign of self-awareness, not failure. The game rewards consistency within whatever trajectory the player has chosen — not adherence to an old plan.

## Design Principles

1. **Real ROI is the only score** — every number must connect to real money or real health
2. **Fast logging** — every daily action under 60 seconds, most under 20
3. **Mobile-first** — designed for phone, enhanced on desktop
4. **No cheating needed** — the game is honest about your real life, not a dopamine trick
5. **Diabetic-aware** — glycemia is a first-class metric, not an afterthought
6. **Single player, full ownership** — no cloud lock-in, exportable always
7. **The game gets harder as you get better** — anti-inflation baked in from day one
8. **Seasons over infinite grind** — 90-day arcs with reflection and reset
9. **Everything is configurable, nothing is hardcoded** — goals adapt to life, not the other way around
10. **The app is a mirror, not a judge** — config changes signal self-awareness, not failure

## XP & Leveling Formula

**Level threshold:** `XP_required = 1000 × level^1.4`

| Level | XP Required | Cumulative |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1,000 | 1,000 |
| 2 | 2,639 | 3,639 |
| 3 | 4,655 | 8,294 |
| 5 | 9,518 | — |
| 10 | 24,943 | — |
| 20 | 66,230 | — |

At current full daily logging (~450 XP/day), Level 5 takes roughly 21 days of perfect play.
Realistically (80% completion) — about 30 days. This is intentional.
